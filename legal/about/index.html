Smojo Builds is a fan-made, unofficial site for Backpack Battles guides, item tools, and a community build creator. It exists to help players learn OP routes and grow the creator’s channel — @SmojoWasTaken.

What you can do here

  • Browse featured and community builds
  • Read deep guide pages with boards, routes, and videos
  • Explore the item catalog with game-style tooltips
  • Draft and submit builds in the creator (including optional Steam history.db runs)
  • Try the unofficial combat sandbox on /sim/ (dummy target, not live PvP)

Combat sandbox

The sandbox is a fan-made dummy fight. It is not official combat, not a replay of ranked matches, and we do not publicly claim it “matches the game” or that it is engine 1:1 until a separate legal/IP review says that marketing is allowed. Personal tinkering on this fan site is fine; selling or advertising game-accurate combat is not, on this stub.
