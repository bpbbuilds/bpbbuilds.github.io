About

Smojo Builds is an unofficial fan project. This page describes the current site and may be updated when features, ownership, or community practices change.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Smojo Builds is a fan-made, unofficial site for Backpack Battles guides, item tools, and a community build creator. It exists to help players learn OP routes and grow the creator’s channel — @SmojoWasTaken.

Not affiliated

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to Backpack Battles or its developers and publishers. All trademarks and game assets belong to their respective owners. Sprites, icons, and UI cues appear here for reference and community guides only.

What you can do here

  • Browse featured and community builds
  • Read deep guide pages with boards, routes, and videos
  • Explore the item catalog with game-style tooltips
  • Draft and submit builds in the creator, including optional Steam history.db runs
  • Sign in with Discord to publish builds, vote, and manage your profile; sign-in returns to the BPB Builds page where it began
  • When site owners enable private access, use may be limited to signed-in members of the BPB Builds Discord server
  • Try the unofficial combat sandbox on /sim/ (dummy target, not live PvP)
  • Optional Premium ($3/mo via Stripe) or founding membership for gated tools

Keeping this page current

We review this overview when a material site feature, account flow, paid offering, data practice, account-access protection, or community policy changes. See the Terms and Privacy pages for the current rules and data details.

Combat sandbox

The sandbox is a fan-made dummy fight. It is not official combat, not a replay of ranked matches, and we do not publicly claim it “matches the game” or that it is engine 1:1 until a separate legal/IP review says that marketing is allowed. Personal tinkering on this fan site is fine; selling or advertising game-accurate combat is not, on this stub.
